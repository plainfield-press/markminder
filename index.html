USPTO filing watch

Someone filed a trademark that sounds like yours last week.

Opposing a confusingly similar filing costs a fraction of what fighting an issued registration costs - but only if you see it during the 30-day opposition window. MarkMinder reads the new USPTO filings every week and flags the ones close to your name, with the serial number and the deadline.
